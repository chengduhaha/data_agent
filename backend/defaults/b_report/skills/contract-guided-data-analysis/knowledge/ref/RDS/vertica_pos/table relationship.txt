# - Use xx as the region placeholder, matching table list.txt.

# - Prefer LEFT JOIN from POS base to enrichment tables to preserve POS rows.
# - Pre-aggregate or rank one-to-many detail tables when the final report must stay at POS order-line grain.

# Notes for tables without a stable direct POS key
# - Reference/config tables such as dim_xx.dim_pub_list_box_detail and ods_xx.ods_dw_prod_dws_dw_param_xref usually join by code_type/code_value or param name/value.
# - Product technical content tables ods_xx.ods_cis_corp_tc_attribute_en, ods_xx.ods_cis_corp_tc_part_technotes_en, and ods_xx.ods_cis_corp_tc_value_en usually join through sku_no or technical attribute identifiers.
# - Finance tables such as dw_xx.dwd_disty_ar_cust_doc_df, dw_xx.dwd_disty_ar_payment_cust_application, dw_xx.dwd_disty_ar_payment_cust_payment, and dw_xx.dwd_disty_ap_hold_df are usually joined by cust_no, vend_no, invoice_no, doc_no, or payment_no depending on the report.
# - Goal/forecast/close tables such as dw_xx.dwd_disty_pm_report_goal, dm_xx.dm_disty_pur_purch_forecast461_rtv2, dm_xx.dm_disty_sales_close_cpo_di, and dw_xx.dws_disty_brpt_cust_mtd are usually joined by report grain keys such as sku_no, vend_no, vpl_no, cust_no, sales_terr, and fiscal period.
