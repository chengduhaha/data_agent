# Notes for usage
# - Profile tables are often filtered by profile_type, profile_cat, active, or profile_no; apply filters before joining when possible.
# - Serial and tracking tables should usually be grouped by order_no, order_type, sku_no or order_no, order_type before joining back to report output.
# - Inventory tables should be filtered to the required inv_type before aggregation; these reports use inv_type in (1, 300) for quantity and inv_type = 1 for ETA.
# - ETA tables should usually be reduced to the minimum or maximum ETA per sku_no, loc_no or order_no, order_type depending on the report.
